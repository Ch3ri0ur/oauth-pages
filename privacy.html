Privacy policy

This application is used only by its owner, with the owner's own Google account.

  • It requests the drive.file permission, which limits access to files the application itself created. It cannot see any other files in Google Drive.
  • Backups are encrypted on the owner's server before upload; the stored files are not readable by Google or anyone else without the owner's keys.
  • No personal data is collected, shared, sold or used for advertising. No analytics, no cookies.
